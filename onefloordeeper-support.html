One Floor Deeper — Support

Need help? Write to birgaripdeveloper@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened; we usually answer within a few days. In the game you can also use Settings → Contact Us.

Questions

I bought gold or a hero, but it did not arrive.

Open the store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Heroes come back this way; if gold is still missing, write to us with the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

On iPhone and iPad your progress is kept in iCloud: sign in with the same Apple Account. On Android, sign in to Google Play Games in the game; your progress is saved to your Google account.

What happens to my loot if I go one floor deeper?

Every floor deeper pays more, but if your hero falls, the loot in your backpack is lost. Take the exit to keep it. Once a mission, an optional ad can bring your hero back with half health or let you leave with half of the backpack.

How do new worlds and the endless building open?

Finish the missions of a world and the next world opens: the apartment, the hospital, the lab, the factory and the museum. Each world also has its own endless building, which opens after one of its missions.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you choose to watch one, for example to double a reward or to revive. In the European Economic Area and the UK you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy Choices.

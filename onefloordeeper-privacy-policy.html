One Floor Deeper — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In short: One Floor Deeper is a game that needs no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. To show ads, count how the game is played, fix crashes and keep your progress, the game uses services from Google and Apple, which process some technical data as described below. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.

Who we are

One Floor Deeper (the "App") is made by Bir Garip Developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: birgaripdeveloper@gmail.com.

What is processed, and why

PurposeDataService
Showing rewarded ads (watching one is always optional) Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID), device and app information, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions Google AdMob
Asking for and storing your ad consent (EEA / UK) Your consent choices Google User Messaging Platform
Understanding how the game is played, to balance and improve it Game events (for example mission started or finished, rewards and purchases), app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location Google Firebase Analytics
Fixing crashes Crash reports (device state and app stack trace at the time of the crash), app instance ID Google Firebase Crashlytics
Notifications, only if you allow them A push token for your device Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service
Leaderboard Your Game Center or Play Games player ID, nickname and best depth score Apple Game Center, Google Play Games Services
Keeping your progress when you reinstall or change phones Your game progress (missions, heroes, gold, materials, upgrades, settings), stored in your own account Apple iCloud, Google Play Games saved games
In-app purchases The product and an order number, to deliver what you bought. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details Apple App Store, Google Play

Your progress and settings are also saved on your device. Each service processes data under its own privacy policy: Google, how Google uses data from apps that use its services, Firebase, Apple.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise (GPS) location.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it except with the services listed above, which work on our behalf or as independent providers of the ad, analytics, game and store services.

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA / UK): answer the consent form when the game first starts; change it later with Settings → Privacy Choices in the game.
  • Tracking on iOS: the game asks for permission to track; you can change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Game Center / Play Games: signing in is optional; without it there is no leaderboard and no cloud save on Android.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK, or KVKK in Türkiye), you may ask to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to its processing. Because we do not know who you are, please include what you need and, for analytics data, the device you play on; we will help as far as the services above allow. Write to birgaripdeveloper@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Data held by the services above is kept according to their retention settings and policies. Progress saved in your iCloud or Google account stays there until you delete it or the game's data from your account. Deleting the App removes the progress saved on your device.

Children

One Floor Deeper is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The effective date above shows the latest version.
